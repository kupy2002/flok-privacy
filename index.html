Flok privacy policy

Effective 18 November 2026 · Kupy Labs

Flok is a photo cleanup app. It sorts a big folder of photos into albums, one for each trip, outing or event, and suggests blurry shots, accidental shots and extra copies you might want to remove.

Flok works entirely on your phone. It has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What Flok reads

With your permission, Flok reads the photos on your phone to analyse them: sharpness, brightness, faces (only to count them and see whether people face the camera and smile; Flok does not recognise who anyone is) and how similar photos are.

When you sort a folder into albums, and only if you allow it, Flok also reads:

  • Where your photos were taken (the location saved in each photo), to tell trips and outings from everyday photos. Places you visit often, like home, are worked out on your phone from your own photos. Flok never shows or names your home, and never looks up places online.
  • Your videos, only so they can move into an album together with the photos taken at the same time. Flok never analyses, judges or removes videos.

Without these permissions Flok still sorts, by date only, and leaves your videos where they are.

What Flok stores, and where

Everything stays in Flok's own storage on your phone:

  • the results of its analysis, so a second scan is faster;
  • a report after each scan, listing each photo and Flok's suggestion;
  • your settings;
  • the list of photos Flok moved to the trash, so you can restore them;
  • after you sort a folder: a record of which photos moved to which album, so you can undo it, and the places you visit often, so sorting works the next time.

None of this leaves your phone, and Flok does not include it in Android's cloud backup. Uninstalling Flok deletes all of it.

Removing photos

Flok never deletes a photo on its own. You review every suggestion first. Photos you remove go to Android's trash, where you can restore them, in Flok or in your gallery, until Android empties the trash (usually after 30 days).

Notifications

If you allow them, Flok shows the progress of a scan, and tells you when suggested albums are ready, in your notifications. Nothing else is sent.

Moving photos into albums

Flok moves photos only after you approve the albums and confirm in Android's own window. Moving changes only the folder a photo is in: nothing is copied, changed or deleted, and you can undo it.

Feedback

If you choose to send feedback, Flok opens your email app or a Google Form in your browser. What you write (and any screenshot you choose to add) goes to Kupy Labs only and is used to improve Flok. Flok itself sends nothing.

Children

Flok is meant for adults and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
